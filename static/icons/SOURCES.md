# Icon sources

The service and app logos below are trademarks of their owners. They are **not** covered by
TheCollector's MIT license, and they're used only to label the service or app a tile stands for.
Don't modify, recolor or crop them.

Where an owner doesn't publish its icon for this kind of use, the tile has a plain icon drawn for
TheCollector instead, and the app's name does the identifying.
